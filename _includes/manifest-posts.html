{% comment %} The pages the Manifest lists, in its build order, as all_posts: for its rows (note.html) and for its search (search.json). Posts pin themselves with the pin emoji and a ¶ in last_modified_at. A page pins itself with pinned: true and the same ¶, because its own emoji would become its SEO image; its row gets the pin. A post with hidden: true stays published, for the links to it, but is not listed. {% endcomment %} {%- assign pinned_pages = site.pages | where_exp: "item", "item.pinned == true" -%} {%- assign listed_posts = site.posts | where_exp: "post", "post.categories contains 'note' or post.categories contains 'testbed'" -%} {%- assign listed_posts = listed_posts | where_exp: "post", "post.hidden != true" -%} {%- assign pinned_posts = listed_posts | where: "emoji", "/emoji/pin.png" -%} {%- assign unpinned_posts = listed_posts | where_exp: "post", "post.emoji != '/emoji/pin.png'" -%} {%- assign all_posts = pinned_pages | concat: pinned_posts | concat: unpinned_posts -%}
