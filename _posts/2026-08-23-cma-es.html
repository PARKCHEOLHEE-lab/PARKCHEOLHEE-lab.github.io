  • Optimizing without a gradient
    • CMA-ES (Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy) minimizes a function \(f\) from its values alone, so it needs no gradient
    • It keeps a normal distribution \(\mathcal{N}(m, \sigma^2 C)\). Every generation samples \(\lambda\) points from it, ranks them by \(f\), and moves the distribution toward the best ones
    • The mean \(m\) says where to search, the step size \(\sigma\) how far, and the covariance \(C\) in which shape

  • Example: the smallest circle
    • Find the smallest circle that holds six points. Search for its center \((x, y)\), so there are two parameters (\(n = 2\))
    • Once the center is fixed, the circle only has to reach the farthest point, so its radius is
      \[ f(x, y) = \max_i \, \lVert (x, y) - p_i \rVert \]
      A smaller \(f\) is a smaller circle
